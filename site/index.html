the problem

Your agent works for twenty minutes at a time.

You shouldn't have to sit and watch it.

how a conversation feels

Illustrative. A scripted example, not a recording.

  1. you › How's the auth refactor going?
  2. talkative › Two of three jobs are done. The third is running in the background, so I'm free to talk.
  3. you › Which files did the second job touch?
  4. talkative › Mostly the session code. In short, it—
  5. you › Never mind. Just tell me when the third one finishes.
  6. talkative › The third job just finished and the tests pass. I'd like to push the branch. Tap Approve on your phone.
  7. you › (taps Approve)

what you get

A conversation, not dictation.

It talks back in plain spoken language, and you can cut in. It summarises decisions and progress. It never reads code aloud. Code and diffs appear on screen for when you look.

It keeps working while you talk.

The coordinator hands long jobs to background subagents and stays free to chat. When a job finishes, it tells you on its own.

Private by default.

Speech runs on your own machine or server, reachable only over your private Tailscale network. No third-party voice service is involved unless you explicitly switch one on.

Built for pockets and bad signal.

It works with the screen locked and earbuds in. It survives tunnels and dropouts, and tells you what you missed when you reconnect.

You stay in control.

Anything that needs your permission waits for you to tap Approve on your phone. If you don't answer in time, the answer is no.

Runs your way.

Split setup: speech on an always-on server, the agent on your computer. Or run everything on one machine.

how it works

Your phone talks to the voice side. Only text goes on to your agent.

privacy

Talkative is private by default, and the agent host runs on your computer. Here is exactly what that means.

Stays on your hardware, by default

  • Your voice audio, unless you switch on a cloud speech engine.
  • Speech recognition and speech output.

What can leave, and when

  • Traffic between your phone and your machines travels over your private Tailscale network.
  • The text of what you say goes to Claude through Claude Code, with your normal login, as any Claude Code prompt does.
  • Your audio, to a cloud speech engine, only if you explicitly switch one on.

status and faq

Status: in development. Not released yet. iPhone only.

Does it work on Android?

Not in the first version. iPhone only.

Is it in the App Store?

No. Early versions are installed by sideloading. App Store distribution may come later.

Do I need a GPU?

No. Speech can run on a CPU server. A GPU makes it faster.

Does my audio go to the cloud?

Not by default. Speech runs on your own hardware. Cloud speech engines can only be enabled explicitly.

Which coding agent?

Claude Code. It uses your normal Claude Code login, and Talkative is not affiliated with Anthropic.

What does it cost?

Talkative is free and open source. You pay for your own Claude usage and for any server you choose to run.

Talkative is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with Anthropic.
